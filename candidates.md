# Candidates — The Funniest Toaster Error Message

Thirteen candidate messages plus one tightening variant were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the winner then defended its place in a revision round.

**Final winner (81 chars):** FAULT: This unit failed three days after your warranty expired. I checked. Twice.

**Runner-up (72 chars):** ERROR: 11,000 breakfasts. Zero thank-yous. The malfunction is unrelated.
